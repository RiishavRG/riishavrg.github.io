UMBC, explained.
Ever wondered who actually does what at U M B C? You’re not alone. Let’s connect the dots, from the president to the people who help with your graduate experience.

President
Start with the president. She leads the university as a whole, setting its direction and bringing senior leaders together. Academic affairs and student affairs are two different parts of that bigger picture.

Provost
The provost is the chief academic officer. Think teaching, academic programs, and the quality of education. The provost works with college deans, the Graduate School, and leaders responsible for other academic areas.

College dean
A college dean leads a particular college. Within that college, departments and graduate programs handle work closer to your everyday experience. Your supervisor, program director, and department have different jobs, too. They’re part of your academic home.

Graduate School
The Graduate School connects graduate education across colleges. Dean Christa Porter leads it. Its teams work on admissions, progress toward graduation, academic advocacy and professional development, and operations. For graduate assistants, this also includes university-level guidance and concerns.

Dean of Students
Now switch to Student Affairs, led by Doctor Renique Kersh. This division supports student life and well-being. Within it, the Dean of Students helps with difficult circumstances and finding support. That’s a different job from leading an academic college or the Graduate School.
