Yes. “Dean” tells you someone leads an area. The rest of the title tells you which one.

Leads an academic college and its departments.

Scope: graduate education across colleges

FROM THE CONVERSATION

Dr. Valerie Sheares Ashby

President, UMBC

Meet & Confer · November 18, 2026
“We are here to work in collaboration and in service to you.”

The context

In her feedback on the Meet & Confer, President Sheares Ashby praised GAAC for bringing real, difficult questions directly to university leadership. She recognized the trust it takes for students to raise those concerns.

What she wanted to emphasize

“We are all trying to do the same thing.”

She also acknowledged that leadership cannot always say yes: funding and other constraints remain. Her message was to keep raising concerns and working together toward solutions, even when every request cannot be granted.
