Password Checking Study

Thank you for taking part. This takes about 5 to 10 minutes.

Before you start

  • Do not use a real password. Make up a new one for this study.
  • Do not use the password you create here for any other account.
  • Do not write your password down or save it in your browser. We will ask you to remember it in about a week.
  • Your password needs at least 12 characters and at least 2 kinds of characters: lowercase letters, uppercase letters, numbers, symbols.

What you will do

  1. Answer a security question.
  2. Choose: see password ideas built from your answers, or make your own password.
  3. Not happy with the ideas? Answer another question, up to 5 in total.
  4. Type your password and check it. Change it until it is Strong.
